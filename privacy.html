2. Our Privacy Principle

Tomera is built on a simple principle: your training and health data stays on your device. Your workout data, cycle data, symptom reports and training history are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's Core Data framework. We operate no server that holds them, and they do not leave your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share them.

A small amount of data can leave your device: usage statistics, only if you turn them on, and anything you actively choose to send us, such as feedback or your email address. Section 5 lists every one of these, and none of them contains health or cycle data.

3. What Data Does Tomera Collect?

All of the following is stored exclusively on your device:

Profile information: Your first name, training goals, equipment preferences, and unit preferences (metric/imperial). This data is entered during onboarding and used to generate your training program.

Cycle data: If you choose to enable cycle tracking, Tomera stores your cycle type, last period start date, cycle length, period length, and adaptation preference. This data is used to estimate your current cycle phase and adapt your training accordingly. It is collected only after you give explicit consent, during onboarding or later in Settings. If you decline, none of it is stored.

Energy level: at each pre-session check-in you rate your energy. Tomera stores it with the workout and uses it to choose your warm-up and adjust the session.

Symptoms and how you feel: if you enable cycle tracking, the check-in also asks about period symptoms and how you feel (for example poor sleep, stress, muscle soreness or PMS), and you can log your period. This is health data, collected under the same explicit consent as your cycle data. Without that consent, the check-in does not ask for it and none of it is stored.

Pain reports: when you report pain during a workout, Tomera can save the report, any note you add and which sets it affected, so that the exercise does not get harder next time. This is health data. It is saved only if you agree the first time you report pain, separately from any other consent. If you don't agree, the report is not kept.

Workout data: Completed workout sessions, including exercises performed, sets, reps, weight used, RPE (rate of perceived exertion) ratings, rest periods, and session duration.

Progression data: Training decisions computed by the progression engine, including weight and rep adjustments, deload decisions, holds after a saved pain report, and cycle-phase adaptations with their explanations.

Consent record: The date and version of each consent you give or withdraw (privacy policy, cycle and symptom data, pain reports, usage statistics), so we can tell you what you agreed to and when.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Profile and workout data, including your energy level: processed on the basis of contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). This data is necessary to generate and adapt your training program, which is the service you signed up for.

Cycle data, symptom reports and check-in health data: this is health data and counts as a special category under Art. 9(1) GDPR. It is processed only on the basis of your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give during onboarding or in Settings before any of it is collected. If you decline, none of it is stored and the app works without it.

You can withdraw this consent at any time in Settings. Withdrawal deletes all stored cycle and symptom data: your cycle profile, your cycle entries, and the symptoms and cycle phase recorded with your workouts. Your workouts themselves stay. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.

Pain reports: this is health data under Art. 9(1) GDPR. It is processed only on the basis of your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give the first time you report pain by choosing "Save". If you choose "Don't save", the report is not kept. You can withdraw this consent at any time in Settings → "Delete pain reports", which deletes all saved pain reports. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.

Usage statistics: processed only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG), which you give on the app's first screen or in Settings. You can withdraw it at any time by turning usage statistics off in Settings.

Feedback submissions and email sign-up: processed on the basis of your consent, given by choosing to submit (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

5. What Data Leaves Your Device?

Very little, and none of it is health or cycle data. There are five cases:

Usage statistics, only if you turn them on: Tomera then records which steps of the setup and of the subscription screen are reached (not what you enter), so we can find the places where the app is confusing or broken. They stay off until you turn them on, on the app's first screen or in Settings. They are processed by TelemetryDeck, a German analytics provider hosting its data in the EU.

What is sent: the name of the step reached, whether the app is a test, TestFlight or App Store version, the version of the app and of the analytics software, and the app's language. Every other technical value the analytics software would send by default (such as device model, iOS version, time zone or usage counts) is sent blank. What is never sent: your cycle data, your symptoms, your pain reports, your phase, your workouts, your name, your email address, or any identifier that persists. When events are sent, TelemetryDeck receives your device's IP address, as every internet connection does. According to TelemetryDeck, it is not stored.

To count a sequence of steps as one visit rather than several, each event carries a random value generated fresh every time the app starts. It is not derived from your device, your Apple Account or anything you entered, and it appears nowhere else in Tomera. It is held in memory, and written to a temporary file on your device while the app is in the background, until the events have been sent. Because of this we cannot identify which statistics came from you, and therefore cannot individually retrieve or delete them (Art. 11(2) GDPR).

While usage statistics are on, the analytics software also keeps a small record on your device of the days and lengths of your app sessions. It is not sent. It is part of your data export and is deleted when you turn usage statistics off or delete all data.

When you turn usage statistics off, nothing more is sent, events not yet sent are discarded, and the analytics software's data on your device is deleted.

Your email address, if you give it: You may optionally give us your email address to hear about updates. This is entirely voluntary, it is never required, and the app is fully usable without it. Email addresses are held by Brevo, an email provider based in the EU.

Your email address is stored separately from everything else and is never connected to your training data, cycle data or usage statistics. We have no way to link an email address to an app installation. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send, which deletes your address from our list.

Feedback submissions (optional): Submitting feedback or contacting Support is voluntary. If you do submit, your message is sent to a Google Form operated by the developer along with diagnostic metadata (app version, iOS version, device model, locale, workout count, days since first launch). For Support submissions, you may optionally include your email for a reply. This data is used solely to improve the app and respond to inquiries.

The form is operated by Google LLC (USA). Submitting feedback therefore transfers your message and the listed diagnostic data to the United States. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission recognises as providing an adequate level of protection. Submitting feedback is entirely voluntary and the app is fully usable without it.

Purchases (Apple): Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Tomera never receives or stores your payment details, billing address, or Apple Account information. The app only receives an anonymous confirmation of whether an active subscription exists. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Crash reports (Apple): If the app crashes, Apple may collect standard crash diagnostics as part of iOS. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy and your device settings, not by Tomera. Tomera adds no crash reporting tool of its own.

6. Recipients and Processors

TelemetryDeck (Germany, hosted in the EU): usage statistics, only if you turn them on. No health data, no persistent identifier.

Brevo (EU): your email address, and only if you choose to give it.

Google LLC (USA, EU-US Data Privacy Framework): feedback messages you choose to send, with the diagnostic metadata listed in §5.

Apple: subscription status and crash diagnostics, governed by Apple's privacy policy.

No other party receives any data. Your cycle data, symptom reports, workouts and training history are received by nobody, because they never leave your device.

7. What Tomera Does NOT Do

  • We do not send your health, cycle, symptom or workout data anywhere. It stays on your device.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or serve ads
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and we ask for no tracking permission
  • We do not use cookies or store any tracking identifier on your device
  • We do not use your data to train machine learning models
  • We do not build a profile of you on any server
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone
  • We do not require an account, a login, or a registration to use the app

8. Your Rights Under GDPR

Right to access: You can export all your data at any time via Settings → "Export Data". This produces a JSON file with everything Tomera stores about you on your device: your profile, your cycle and symptom logs, your pain reports, your training plan, your full workout history, Tomera's record of each training decision, your consent record and, if usage statistics are on, the analytics software's record.

Right to rectification: You can correct your profile, cycle, and training data at any time in Settings.

Right to erasure: You can delete all your data at any time via Settings → "Delete All Data". This permanently removes all data from your device. Since Tomera stores none of it externally, deletion is complete and irreversible. To remove your email address, use the unsubscribe link in any email we send.

Right to restriction: You can disable cycle adaptation in Settings. Your cycle data is then no longer used to modify your training, while the data itself remains stored.

Right to data portability: The JSON export format allows you to take your data to any other service.

Right to object: You can disable cycle adaptation at any time in Settings without deleting your data.

Right to withdraw consent: You can withdraw each consent at any time in Settings: for cycle and symptom data, for pain reports with "Delete pain reports", and for usage statistics by turning them off. §4 describes what each withdrawal deletes.

On usage statistics: as described in §5, they carry no identifier that persists or that can be linked to you, so we are unable to locate, retrieve or delete the statistics originating from any particular person (Art. 11(2) GDPR). The analytics software's record on your device is part of your export and is deleted when you turn usage statistics off.

10. Data Retention

On your device: your data persists for as long as the app is installed. Deleting the app removes all of it. There is no cloud backup of Tomera data unless you have enabled iCloud device backups through Apple (which is governed by Apple's privacy policy).

Usage statistics: kept by TelemetryDeck as individual events without any persistent identifier, so they cannot be attributed to any person. They are deleted once they are no longer needed to evaluate the setup and subscription screens, at the latest after 24 months.

Your email address: retained until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove it.

Feedback submissions: retained for as long as needed to respond and to act on the feedback.
