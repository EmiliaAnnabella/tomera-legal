2. Our Privacy Principle

Tomera is built on a simple principle: your data stays on your device. We do not operate servers, databases, or cloud infrastructure that store your personal information. Your workout data, cycle data, health information, and training history are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's Core Data framework and do not leave your device, unless you explicitly choose to export or share them.

3. What Data Does Tomera Collect?

All of the following data is stored exclusively on your device:

Profile information: Your first name, training goals, equipment preferences, and unit preferences (metric/imperial). This data is entered during onboarding and used to generate your training program.

Hormonal and cycle data: If you choose to enable cycle tracking, Tomera stores your cycle type, last period start date, cycle length, period length, and adaptation preference. This data is used to estimate your current cycle phase and adapt your training accordingly. It is collected only after you give explicit consent during onboarding. If you decline, none of it is stored.

Workout data: Completed workout sessions, including exercises performed, sets, reps, weight used, RPE (rate of perceived exertion) ratings, rest periods, and session duration. Pre-session check-in data including energy levels and symptom reports are also stored.

Progression data: Training decisions computed by the progression engine, including weight and rep adjustments, deload decisions, and cycle-phase adaptations with their explanations.

Consent record: The date and version of the privacy policy you consented to, so we can tell you what you agreed to and when.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Profile and workout data: processed on the basis of contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). This data is necessary to generate and adapt your training program, which is the service you signed up for.

Cycle and hormonal data: this is health data and counts as a special category under Art. 9(1) GDPR. It is processed only on the basis of your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give during onboarding before any cycle data is collected. If you decline, no cycle data is stored and the app works without it.

You can withdraw this consent at any time in Settings. Withdrawal deletes all stored cycle data and does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Feedback submissions: processed on the basis of your consent, given by choosing to submit (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

5. What Data Leaves Your Device?

Almost nothing. There are only three exceptions:

Feedback submissions (optional): Submitting feedback or contacting Support is voluntary. If you do submit, your message is sent to a Google Form operated by the developer along with diagnostic metadata (app version, iOS version, device model, locale, workout count, days since first launch). For Support submissions, you may optionally include your email for a reply. This data is used solely to improve the app and respond to inquiries.

The form is operated by Google LLC (USA). Submitting feedback therefore transfers your message and the listed diagnostic data to the United States. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission recognises as providing an adequate level of protection. Submitting feedback is entirely voluntary and the app is fully usable without it.

Purchases (Apple): Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Tomera never receives or stores your payment details, billing address, or Apple Account information. The app only receives an anonymous confirmation of whether an active subscription exists. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Crash reports (Apple): If the app crashes, Apple may collect standard crash diagnostics as part of iOS. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy and your device settings, not by Tomera. Tomera does not add any additional analytics, tracking, or crash reporting tools.

6. What Tomera Does NOT Do

  • We do not use analytics services (no Google Analytics, no Firebase, no Mixpanel)
  • We do not track your behavior, location, or usage patterns
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties. The only external transmissions are voluntary feedback submissions you explicitly initiate (described in §5).
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or serve ads
  • We do not use your data to train machine learning models
  • We do not create user profiles on external servers
  • We do not require an account to use the app

7. Your Rights Under GDPR

Right to access: You can export all your data at any time via Settings → "Export Data". This produces a complete JSON file containing all data Tomera stores about you.

Right to rectification: You can correct your profile, cycle, and training data at any time in Settings.

Right to erasure: You can delete all your data at any time via Settings → "Delete All Data". This permanently removes all data from your device. Since Tomera does not store data externally, deletion is complete and irreversible.

Right to restriction: You can disable cycle adaptation in Settings. Your cycle data is then no longer used to modify your training, while the data itself remains stored.

Right to data portability: The JSON export format allows you to take your data to any other service.

Right to object: You can disable cycle adaptation at any time in Settings without deleting your data.

Right to withdraw consent: You can withdraw your consent to cycle data processing at any time in Settings. This deletes all stored cycle data.

9. Data Retention

Your data persists on your device for as long as the app is installed. Deleting the app removes all data. There is no cloud backup of Tomera data unless you have enabled iCloud device backups through Apple (which is governed by Apple's privacy policy).

10. Children's Privacy

Tomera is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.
